Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

This classic Italian dish is a simple yet delicious combination of spaghetti, eggs, and crispy pancetta. It's a crowd-pleaser that's perfect for any occasion!

Ingredients

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 4 strips bacon, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, cook the bacon until crispy. Remove and set aside.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, Parmesan cheese, garlic, pepper, and salt.
  4. Drain the cooked spaghetti and add it to the skillet with the bacon.
  5. Remove the skillet from heat and quickly stir in the egg mixture until creamy.
  6. Serve immediately with additional Parmesan cheese and black pepper.
